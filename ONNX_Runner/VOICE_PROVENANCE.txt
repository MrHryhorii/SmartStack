Bundled voice embedding provenance
=================================

The project author supplied the corresponding source WAV recordings. All
15 WAVs and the 15 .voice embeddings are included in Voices/ and copied to
published builds. Their filenames match. This naming is the available
pairing record; re-extraction was not performed to verify that each
embedding was made from the corresponding WAV.

The project identifies LibriTTS-R as the source of 13 voices:
https://www.openslr.org/141/ (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original corpus paths and
individual speaker IDs were not retained. Recordings were selected and
renamed; the source audio was transformed into .voice embeddings. Dataset
creators and the paper are credited in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. These
voice names do not identify or claim endorsement by individual speakers.

For male and female, the project author identified these Freesound recordings:
female: vero.marengere, "French Canadian Woman Giving Instructions 04.wav"
  https://freesound.org/people/vero.marengere/sounds/514877/
male: aarongbuk, "other radio stations.wav"
  https://freesound.org/people/aarongbuk/sounds/222599/
Both source pages display Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Attribution is not
required by CC0, but these links preserve the known provenance. The bundled
female.wav matches the page's listed 8-second, 48-kHz, stereo 24-bit format;
male.wav matches its approximately 9.67-second, 48-kHz, stereo 32-bit format.
The original downloads were not available here for byte-for-byte comparison,
so the pairing relies on the author's identification and matching metadata.
