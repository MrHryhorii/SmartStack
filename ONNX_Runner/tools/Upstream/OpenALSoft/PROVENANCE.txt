Adaptation: mono early/late reverb, two-tap echo, integer delay and biquads.
Profiles, intensity, return balance and final peak protection are caller-owned.
Echo damping is bounded below Nyquist; extended delays support sparse echoes.
No OpenAL binary or new runtime package is used.
